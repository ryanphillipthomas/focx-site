Focx makes AI tools for learning, built to help you be a better human. Focx is operated by Ryan Thomas, doing business as ryanthomas.ai ("we," "us").

This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we only have your email address, we use it to tell you when there's something real to try, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address. That's it. We don't ask for your name, and we don't sell or rent your information.

We don't use analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking on focx.ai. Like any website, the companies that run our servers automatically process basic technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don't use that information to profile you.

Why we collect it

We use your email address for one thing: to email you when Focx has something real for you to try. We won't send newsletters or marketing filler, and we won't use your email for anything else without asking you first.

Who else sees it

We store waitlist emails in our own database on Cloudflare. Our website is hosted by [[HOSTING: Render or Cloudflare, confirm]]. When we send the launch email, we'll use [[EMAIL SENDING SERVICE]]. These providers handle data on our behalf and can't use it for their own purposes.

We may disclose information if the law requires it. If Focx is ever sold or merged, the waitlist could transfer to the new owner, and this policy would still apply to it.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Either way, we delete waitlist emails within 12 months after Focx launches, unless you've signed up for Focx.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe: Every email we send has an unsubscribe link.
  • Delete or access your data: Email privacy@focx.ai and we'll delete your email address or tell you what we hold. We'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK, or California), you may have additional rights, such as correcting your data or objecting to how it's used. Contact us and we'll honor them. Where EU or UK law applies, we rely on your consent to process your email, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Children

Focx's waitlist isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change meaningfully affects how we use your email, we'll tell you before it takes effect.
